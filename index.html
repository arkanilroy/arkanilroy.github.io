About

I'm a postdoctoral researcher in the Klem Research Group at Louisiana State University, where I work on computational approaches to understanding and engineering enzyme function. Our lab combines quantum mechanical modeling, molecular dynamics simulation, and machine learning to study catalytic mechanisms and design novel biocatalysts.

I'm broadly interested in bridging fundamental theory with practical applications in medicine, biotechnology, and sustainability — using computation to answer questions that are hard to reach experimentally alone.

Research interests

Enzyme catalysis

Understanding the mechanisms that make enzymes such efficient, selective catalysts.

Quantum mechanics & MD

Modeling reaction pathways and conformational dynamics at atomic resolution.

Machine learning

Applying ML methods to accelerate biocatalyst design and mechanistic discovery.

Biocatalyst design

Engineering novel enzyme systems for applications in sustainability and biotech.
